'kimaki': minor

Quote short earlier assistant text and the run footer in Discord.

Kimaki holds only unfinished text. Tool parts still flush as they run. Completed text of at most two lines is sent immediately as a Discord quote. Longer text, callouts, and text flushed because of a question, sleep, or action-button tool stay full width. The completion footer is also quoted.

This applies to live sessions, `/resume`, `/fork`, and external OpenCode sync.
